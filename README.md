# NexHaven Systems Website

Static GitHub Pages website for NexHaven Systems' Home Services Revenue Operations offer.

## Current funnel

- Homepage: managed-operations positioning and 30-Day Revenue Recovery Pilot
- Vertical pages: HVAC, plumbing, and roofing
- Assessment: aggregate Revenue Leakage Assessment with UTM/click-ID capture
- Submission fallback: prefilled email to info@nexhavenos.com

## Before enabling paid traffic

1. Add the approved CRM or automation webhook to `config.js`.
2. Add the approved analytics container or measurement ID.
3. Test end-to-end lead receipt, notification, assignment, and response time.
4. Have qualified counsel review the privacy policy and outreach practices.

Do not collect customer names, invoice details, payment information, or other sensitive account data through the public assessment.
