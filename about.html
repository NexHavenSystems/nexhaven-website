About LeadCo

A focused approach to lead partnerships.

LeadCo is a DBA of NexHaven Systems LLC. Its operating model brings lead generation, contact-center qualification, and distribution together around written buyer requirements.

Buyer first.
Outcome measured.

The work starts with a buyer and a defined campaign. Sources and qualification follow the specification, and results are reconciled to actual acceptance and payment.

  1. 01
    Clear products

    Leads, qualified inbound calls, live transfers, and appointments when specifically accepted.

  2. 02
    Honest inventory

    Fresh inquiries and aged records are described separately. Volume and performance are not assumed.

  3. 03
    Accountable outcomes

    Accepted deliveries, returns, payment, and cost determine whether a campaign works.
