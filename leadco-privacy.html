Business inquiry privacy

How we handle business inquiries.

This notice covers LeadCo lead, customer support, insurance recovery and medical billing inquiries.

Who operates this site

NexHaven Systems LLC dba LeadCo. Correspondence: 7901 4th St N, Ste 300, St. Petersburg, FL 33702. Contact: atlas@nexhavenos.com.

What happens when you use the form

The form prepares a summary in your browser. It does not automatically send your form entries to us or save them in browser storage. You can review and download the summary or open it in your email application. We receive the inquiry only after you send the email.

Information we receive

Your email may include your company, business contact details, service interests, geography, staffing or volume needs, and the requirements you choose to share. We use these details to respond, assess fit, and manage the requested business relationship. Authorized operators may record relevant business details in our restricted business CRM.

Callback requests

The callback form prepares an email with your business contact details, preferred time, time zone and service interest. We receive it only when you send that email. We use the details to arrange the call you requested. A preferred time is not a confirmed appointment and a callback request does not subscribe you to marketing texts.

Service providers

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Business email and CRM records use Google services. These providers may process information under their service terms and privacy notices. Website fonts may load from Google Fonts. This site does not use advertising pixels or send your form entries to an advertising analytics provider.

Access and retention

Access to inquiries is limited to authorized business operators. We retain information as needed to manage the inquiry and any resulting business relationship, subject to applicable recordkeeping obligations. Contact us to request correction, deletion, or a restriction on use; we may need to verify the request.

Business information only

Do not include consumer lead lists, loan applications, bank details, government identifiers, patient information, or customer account records. Inventory transfers and access to client systems require a separate agreed process. An inquiry does not authorize consumer marketing or confirm a campaign, staffing engagement, or lead order.
