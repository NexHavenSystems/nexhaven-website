Compliance & quality

A clear standard, from source to sale.

Our campaign framework starts with documented requirements and follows the record through qualification, delivery, and reconciliation.

Source & permission review

Review source pages, exact consent versions, timestamps, allowed recipients and channels, certificates where required, and revocation records.

Eligibility & duplication

Evaluate the buyer’s required fields, location, product, lead age, duplicate window, and previous sale restrictions.

Calls & transfers

Agree on qualification questions, disclosures, permitted recording, hours, and capacity. Preserve transfer context and disposition.

Acceptance & reconciliation

Record delivery receipts, accepted or rejected outcomes, return reasons, invoices, collected payment, and campaign costs.

Campaign approval comes first.

Specific source, consent, privacy, recording, and delivery controls must be reviewed and implemented for the approved campaign. This framework is not a claim of certification or a guarantee that every submitted record is eligible.
